# SIM Archetypes

Archetypes are recurring patterns the coach can notice. They should be treated as hypotheses, not diagnoses.

## Drift

The company keeps moving, but the work no longer clearly serves the current constraint.

Signals:

- many active tasks, weak strategic link
- agents producing artifacts nobody reviews
- plans expand faster than learning

## Delay

The company keeps collecting information but avoids commitment.

Signals:

- research loops without decisions
- approvals stay vague
- customer contact is deferred

## Over-Control

The human keeps agents on a leash so short that leverage disappears.

Signals:

- every small task requires approval
- agents cannot gather context or propose drafts
- operator fatigue rises while output stays manual

## Unchecked Acceleration

Agents execute faster than the company can judge, learn, or govern.

Signals:

- public claims, spend, or outreach happen without review
- many outputs appear, few are integrated
- the system feels productive but incoherent

## Coach Rule

If an archetype appears, phrase it as a question: "This looks a little like unchecked acceleration. Do you want to add a review point before the next step?"
