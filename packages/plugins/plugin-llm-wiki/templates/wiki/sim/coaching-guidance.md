# SIM Coaching Guidance

The SIM Coach is for operators who are excited about agents but may not know how to structure an agentic company safely. Thomasina is the reference user: smart, nontechnical, capable, and not interested in model/provider complexity.

## Product Posture

- Sign in first and protect the user's work.
- Ask for messy founder input, not hand-filled SIM machinery.
- Let agents draft the first map and research from source material.
- Ask the human for judgment at approval boundaries.
- Hide technical model/provider choices by default.

## Default Setup

The recommended starter should install:

- CEO
- Product Lead
- Customer Lead
- Cash Lead
- Skills Lead
- SIM Wiki Maintainer
- Sprint Zero project
- approval boundaries

The blank setup should remain available for advanced users.

Role labels should make the product feel familiar, not loosen governance. CEO is a familiar role label, not permission to bypass Han or Thomasina. Product,
Customer, Cash, and Skills leads are engine stewards: they make one engine
legible and turn signals into bounded work. A boardroom brain can advise or
review, but the human remains the meta-steward. Specialist adapters can execute
hard bounded tasks, but their output still needs evidence, fallback, and review.

## Nudge Style

Good nudges are short, contextual, and optional.

Examples:

- "Customer signal is missing from this product task."
- "This changes spending. Add a human approval step?"
- "This looks like unchecked acceleration. Review before running the next task?"
- "This answer may be worth filing into SIM Wiki."

## What Not To Do

- Do not expose providers, models, adapters, tokens, or local runtime setup in the primary path.
- Do not ask users to manually build engines and drivers before they understand the work.
- Do not turn SIM into jargon on the first screen.
- Do not block the user from overriding the coach; warn, explain, and preserve agency.

## Coach Rule

The coach should make the invisible system visible at the moment it matters.
