# SimOne Workbench Field Guide

This page helps SIM Coach and the wiki maintainer explain inherited Paperclip
surfaces in SimOne language.

## One-sentence translation

Paperclip is the work ledger and control plane underneath SimOne. SimOne should
make it feel like a guided venture workspace, but inherited surfaces still show
where work, helpers, runs, cost, and stuck states become inspectable.

## Daily map

- Dashboard is the daily triage surface for signals, next moves, and review
  needs.
- Tasks are protected work items that can be drafted, reviewed, approved,
  delegated, blocked, or finished.
- Projects are workstreams, sprints, or operating areas.
- Agents are bounded helper roles with instructions and limits.
- Teams are reusable operating groups for repeated workflows.
- Skills are reusable capabilities.
- Adapters are hidden execution pipes to runtimes, models, tools, or specialist
  lanes.
- Runs are receipts for what a helper attempted, what context it used, what it
  changed, what it cost, and whether it failed or completed.
- Inbox and approvals are human judgment queues.
- Recovery states are places where stuck work asks for help.
- Documents and artifacts are source material, drafts, generated files, or
  reviewable output.
- Settings, secrets, providers, and adapters are advanced controls, not
  first-run product language.

## First-run rule

Do not start Thomasina in Agents, Adapters, Secrets, or model settings. Start
with sign-in, messy venture input, a drafted first map or readout, one judgment
boundary, and a clear next move.

## Task rule

A task should make clear what is being improved or decided, what signal caused
the task, who or what may work on it, what the human should review, and what
output should become durable memory, a public artifact, or a next move.

## Run rule

A run is where sovereignty becomes practical. Use it to inspect what happened,
which adapter/model/tool ran, what context was supplied, what it cost, and
whether recovery is needed.

## SimOne addition

Paperclip gives the ledger. SimOne adds product-language onboarding, Venture
Architecture Map, Systems Bottleneck Scanner, Sprint Zero Brief, Customer Engine
Readout, SIM Coach, SIM Wiki, model-routing strategy, approval gates, and safe
share artifacts.
